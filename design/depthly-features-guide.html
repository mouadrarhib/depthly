The features, and how they fit

Home · Your next useful step

See today’s plan, choose a task, and prepare a focus session. Recent sessions, your daily goal, and weekly focus keep your progress close.

How it connects: Planner supplies today’s tasks. Selecting one gives the timer its project and task context.

Projects · A home for related work

Keep a client, course, or goal in a project. Search, filter, and sort projects; switch between list and card views. Open a project to see its tasks and recorded sessions. Archive projects you no longer need in your active workspace.

How it connects: One project contains many tasks and can accumulate many focus sessions.

Tasks · Make the next action clear

Give a task a title, priority, optional deadline, and planning dates. Use To do, In progress, and Done. List view helps scan work; the Pro Kanban board supports moving tasks between columns.

How it connects: A task belongs to one project. It can have several focus sessions before you mark it done.

Planner · Decide when you intend to work

Use Today, Week, or Month. Set a planning window, assign a work day, or pick from suggested tasks. Suggestions come from unscheduled tasks inside the selected day’s planning window, ordered by deadline and priority.

How it connects: Still to do keeps unfinished scheduled work visible. Nothing silently reschedules your task.

Repeating tasks · Set the rhythm once

Repeat every day, on weekdays, or on selected weekdays, with a start date and optional end date. Mark a particular day done or skipped without manually creating another task.

How it connects: One repeating task has dated occurrences. Finishing the whole task stops future occurrences until it is reopened.

Timer · Give one task your attention

Use Pomodoro, a custom countdown, or a free stopwatch. Pause, resume, finish, use fullscreen, and adjust your focus/break rhythm. Choose a project and task when you want time attributed to that work.

How it connects: One active timer run is restored after reload and synced across your signed-in tabs and devices. Choosing a task does not automatically start the timer.

Sessions · A record of the time you spent

Browse focus sessions and breaks. Search and filter by project, date, duration, and type. Open details or edit a session’s title, notes, project, and task.

How it connects: Recorded timing stays fixed. Personal focus totals come from saved focus sessions; breaks are kept separate.

Goals & streaks · See steady effort add up

Set a daily focus target and follow the time recorded toward it. Streak indicators help you see consistency across days. Goal completion and task completion are different signals.

How it connects: Your daily goal measures focus time across projects. Filtering analytics to one project does not narrow the overall goal.

Analytics · Understand where your time went

Overview shows the bigger picture. Daily explores one day; Weekly compares days; Monthly combines a calendar and day details; Yearly shows activity and monthly patterns. Filter by project.

How it connects: Focus sessions feed time totals, project breakdowns, activity days, and history. Free accounts have a seven-day analytics window.

Share & export · Take your progress with you

Share a supported Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly analytics view as a branded image. Pro and Lifetime accounts can also export session records as CSV, with date/project filters and optional breaks.

How it connects: A progress image is a visual summary. CSV is a set of records; it is not an invoice or a client billing system.

Private groups · Focus alongside people you invite

Create a private focus circle, invite friends or teammates, and choose daily, seven-day, or monthly rounds. Members see the group’s ranking and optional per-member target.

How it connects: Only eligible trusted focus time recorded during the round and after joining contributes. Group membership does not make your profile public.

Account & billing · Keep your workspace yours

Manage your profile, avatar, optional public profile, timer preferences, email/password, and account controls. Billing explains your current plan, plan history, and available upgrade or cancellation actions.

How it connects: Free, Pro, and Lifetime change limits and access. Lifetime is a one-time plan; Pro is renewable. Receipts come through purchase emails.

What Depthly keeps separate

Time spent is not the same as work finished

A saved focus session adds time. The task remains open until you mark it done. For a repeating task, a daily checkbox affects that occurrence, not every future day.

A visible reminder is not a scheduled notification

Today’s plan and Still to do surface the work to consider. This version does not send scheduled browser or push reminders.

A private group is not a public leaderboard

Groups are invite-only and available now. The public leaderboard is hidden from the current app; its code and data remain preserved.

A session record is not an editable timesheet entry

Server timing determines recorded duration. You can edit descriptive metadata and work links, but saved timing is immutable. Personal analytics can include older legacy records; private group scoring uses eligible trusted sessions.

The light theme is a proposal

The current app is dark. The separate light-mode HTML is a design reference for later development, not a shipped theme switch.

Privacy in plain language

Your workspace data is tied to your signed-in account. Database access policies scope projects, tasks, sessions, and recurring-day records to their owner. Private group rankings require membership; the invite preview exposes only limited group information. Public profiles are optional.

Timer records and multi-record updates use server functions so time and related totals stay consistent. This guide explains product behavior; it is not a security audit.
